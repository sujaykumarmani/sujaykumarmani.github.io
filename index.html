Profile

Understand the books.
Improve the work.

I am a core accountant by background, with experience that extends into financial analysis, FP&A, reporting and finance-process improvement.

My career started with the fundamentals: processing transactions, checking entries, reconciling accounts and helping close the books correctly. Since then, I have worked across Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable/RTR, General Ledger, intercompany accounting, fixed assets, accruals, month-end close and management reporting.

I look beyond completing a task. I want to understand how the process works, where errors or unnecessary manual effort happen, and how the team can make the work simpler and more reliable. I can work at transaction level and explain the impact on Cost Centres, the Balance Sheet and the P&L.
